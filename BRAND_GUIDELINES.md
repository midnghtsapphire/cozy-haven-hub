# duskglow Brand Guidelines

*Version 1.0 — May 2026*

## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 Mission

> Make every desk feel like a sanctuary.

duskglow exists to help people transform their workspace into a place they genuinely want to be — thoughtfully lit, beautifully organized, and quietly comforting. Every product we sell is chosen because it earns its place on a desk, not just in a cart.

### 1.2 Vision

To become the defining DTC brand of the cozy productivity generation — the brand that creators, students, and remote workers reach for when they want their workspace to tell a story.

### 1.3 Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Intentionality** | Every product is curated, not collected. We say no to things that don't fit the sanctuary aesthetic — even if they'd sell. |
| **Warmth** | We don't talk like a corporation. We talk like a friend who has great taste and genuinely wants your desk to be beautiful. |
| **Quality** | We'd rather sell fewer products that last than more products that feel cheap after a week. |
| **Community** | Our customers are not just buyers — they are desk enthusiasts who make content, share setups, and inspire each other. We build for them. |
| **Accessibility** | Beautiful workspaces aren't a luxury. We price intentionally so our entry tier is within reach for students. |

## 2. Visual Identity

### 2.1 Color Palette

The duskglow color system is called the **Dreamy Sanctuary Palette**. All colors use HSL values to maintain flexibility across light and dark surfaces.

#### Primary Colors

| Name | HSL | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Blush** | `hsl(350 45% 75%)` | Primary buttons, accents, active states |
| **Blush Light** | `hsl(350 50% 92%)` | Hover states, subtle backgrounds, badges |
| **Lavender** | `hsl(280 30% 85%)` | Section backgrounds, tag chips, muted accents |
| **Lavender Deep** | `hsl(280 25% 70%)` | Secondary text accents, icon fills |

#### Supporting Colors

| Name | HSL | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Cream** | `hsl(35 35% 95%)` | Page background (light mode), card surfaces |
| **Sage** | `hsl(145 25% 80%)` | Success states, eco/nature accents, tags |
| **Plum** | `hsl(280 20% 25%)` | Primary text, dark backgrounds |
| **Glow** | `hsl(35 60% 90%)` | Warm highlight, shimmer effects, ambient glow |

#### Gradients

| Name | Definition | Usage |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Hero** | `linear-gradient(135deg, hsl(350 50% 92%), hsl(280 30% 92%), hsl(35 35% 95%))` | Hero sections, landing banners |
| **Card** | `linear-gradient(180deg, hsl(30 20% 99%), hsl(350 40% 97%))` | Product card backgrounds |
| **Glow** | `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, hsl(350 45% 85% / 0.4), transparent 70%)` | Background ambient effects |

#### Dark Mode

In dark mode, backgrounds shift to `hsl(280 20% 8%)` (deep plum-black). Colors invert and desaturate slightly to maintain warmth without harshness. The aesthetic should feel like a "candlelit room at night" — never cold blue-grey.

#### Accessibility Note

All text/background color combinations must meet **WCAG 2.2 AA** contrast minimums (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text). The Plum on Cream combination achieves approximately 9.5:1 and is the preferred pairing for body copy.

### 2.2 Typography

duskglow uses a two-typeface system that combines editorial warmth with clean readability.

#### Cormorant Garamond — Serif Display

- **Source:** Google Fonts
- **Weights used:** 400 (Regular), 400 Italic, 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold)
- **Role:** Headlines, product names, editorial pull-quotes, hero callouts
- **Character:** Elegant, dreamy, high-end — evokes the feel of an independent boutique or curated magazine

```
font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif;
```

| Use | Specification |
|-----|--------------|
| H1 / Hero headline | 48–72px, weight 400–500, slight letter-spacing |
| H2 / Section headline | 32–40px, weight 500 |
| H3 / Product title | 24–28px, weight 500 |
| Pull quotes | 20–24px, weight 400 Italic |

#### Outfit — Sans-Serif Body

- **Source:** Google Fonts
- **Weights used:** 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold)
- **Role:** Body copy, UI labels, navigation, buttons, form inputs, descriptions
- **Character:** Modern and approachable — clean without feeling cold

```
font-family: 'Outfit', system-ui, sans-serif;
```

| Use | Specification |
|-----|--------------|
| Body / descriptions | 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6 |
| UI labels / nav | 14–15px, weight 500 |
| Buttons | 14px, weight 600, tracking-wide |
| Captions / meta | 12–13px, weight 400 |

#### Do Not Use

- System serif fonts in branded communications (use Cormorant Garamond)
- More than two typefaces in a single layout
- Bold in body copy except for inline emphasis on specific values

### 2.3 Iconography

- **Library:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev) — open-source, consistent stroke weight
- **Style:** Outline icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps
- **Size:** 16px for inline/UI, 20–24px for feature icons, 32px+ for empty states
- **Color:** Icons inherit text color unless used as a decorative accent (then use Lavender or Blush)

### 2.4 Imagery & Photography Style

**Subject matter:** Real desk setups, products in context (not floating on white), ambient lighting scenarios, hands arranging objects, close-ups of textures.

**Lighting:** Always warm-toned (golden hour, candlelight, desk lamp glow). Never harsh white flash.

**Color treatment:** Slightly desaturated highlights, warm shadows, no heavy filters. The photo should feel like the room is softly glowing.

**Composition:** Off-center subjects, intentional negative space, visible textures (wood, fabric, washi tape, paper).

**What to avoid:**
- Pure white studio product shots (feels cold and generic)
- Overly stylized flat lays that feel inaccessible
- Cluttered messy desks (we sell sanctuary, not chaos)
- Neon or cool-toned lighting

**Aspect ratios:** 1:1 for product grids, 4:5 for Instagram feed, 16:9 for hero banners, 9:16 for Reels/TikTok.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

### 3.1 Brand Personality

duskglow speaks like a **trusted friend with impeccable taste** — someone who DM'd you a lamp they saw and said "this would look so good on your desk" before you even knew you wanted it.

Four words that define the duskglow voice:

- **Warm** — caring, not clinical
- **Dreamy** — evocative and visual, not dry
- **Grounded** — honest about what a product is and isn't; never overpromises
- **Encouraging** — we believe in your right to have a beautiful workspace

### 3.2 Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Product descriptions | Sensory, evocative | "The kind of lamp that makes you want to stay at your desk after 9pm." |
| Checkout / confirmation | Warm, efficient | "Your sanctuary is on its way ✨" |
| Error messages | Honest, light | "Something went sideways — let's try that again." |
| Email subject lines | Curious, specific | "Your sanctuary archetype is ready →" |
| Social captions | Playful, visual | "soft glow. warm focus. this is the one." |
| Customer support | Patient, empathetic | Never rushing, always solution-first |
| Admin / technical | Clear, neutral | No brand voice needed — be precise |

### 3.3 Words We Use

| Preferred | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| sanctuary | Our core concept; use freely |
| curated, intentional | Signals quality selection |
| glow, soft, warm, cozy | Sensory, on-brand |
| your desk, your setup | Ownership; personal |
| we | One brand, one voice |

### 3.4 Words We Avoid

| Avoid | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| "cheap", "budget" | Undercuts quality positioning |
| "luxury" | Overstates and excludes |
| "innovative" | Generic tech-speak; not our world |
| "synergy", "leverage" | Corporate; wrong register entirely |
| ALL CAPS for emphasis | Feels aggressive |
| Exclamation marks in product names | Cheapens the aesthetic |

### 3.5 Inclusive Language

- Write for everyone, not a specific gender identity
- Avoid assuming the customer's relationship type (don't default to "boyfriend gift")
- "desk setup" is more inclusive than "gaming setup" or "work setup" (we serve both)
- Acknowledge that "cozy" includes neurodivergent folks seeking sensory-friendly workspaces

## 4. Product Naming Conventions

Product names should evoke mood, texture, or light quality — not be clinical descriptions.

**Good:** "Blush Hour Lamp", "Moss Tray Collection", "Quiet Glow Bundle"

**Avoid:** "Desk Organizer Type A", "LED Lamp SKU-2204", "Bundle Pack #3"

Category names follow the same principle:
- Lighting (not "Lamps")
- Organizers (not "Storage")
- Comfort (not "Accessories")
- Bundles (not "Packages" or "Kits")

## 5. Visual Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do

- Pair Cormorant Garamond headlines with Outfit body text
- Use the full gradient palette in hero sections and large banners
- Leave breathing room — don't crowd components
- Use real-world photography whenever possible alongside product shots
- Ensure all interactive elements have visible focus rings (accessibility)
- Animate with subtlety: fade, float, shimmer — nothing jarring

### ❌ Don't

- Use more than 3 colors in a single card or section
- Mix Blush and Sage as co-primaries on the same page section (they compete)
- Use Plum as a background color in light mode (reserved for dark mode)
- Add drop shadows heavier than `shadow-elevated`
- Place text directly on photographic backgrounds without a scrim or overlay
- Use all-caps for any body copy or button label longer than 2 words

## 6. Accessibility Standards

duskglow is committed to **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** compliance.

- All form inputs use floating labels with both placeholder and visible label text
- Color contrast meets 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text
- All interactive elements are keyboard-navigable with visible focus states
- Screen reader compatibility: semantic HTML, ARIA labels on icon-only buttons
- Star ratings are implemented with accessible labels (not icon-only)
- Cart and checkout flows use `<fieldset>` and `<legend>` for grouped inputs
- No element flashes more than 3 times per second

## 7. Asset Guidelines

All brand assets (logo files, font licenses, product photography) are stored in the private `duskglow-brand-assets` repository.

**File formats:**
- Logo: SVG (primary), PNG at 1x/2x/3x (for social and print)
- Product photography: WebP (web), TIFF (print), minimum 1200px on shortest side
- Icons: Lucide SVG (do not rasterize icons below 48px)
