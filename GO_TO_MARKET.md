# Go-to-Market Strategy — duskglow

**Curated desk sanctuary essentials for the cozy productivity generation.**

---

## Executive Summary

duskglow is a direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand selling ambient lamps, textured desk organizers, comfort accessories, and curated workspace bundles. The platform targets a growing segment of work-from-home professionals, students, and desk aesthetic enthusiasts who are willing to pay a premium for products that make their workspace feel intentional and calming.

The cozy desk aesthetic is a proven, high-engagement category on social platforms: #desksetup has over 5 billion views on TikTok and Pinterest "cozy home office" searches have grown 140% year-over-year. duskglow is positioned to capture this demand with a curated, premium-feeling DTC brand.

---

## Market Research

### Target Market

**Primary — Cozy Desk Enthusiasts (18–35)**
- Students and young professionals who spend 6–12 hours a day at a desk
- Highly active on Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube (#desksetup, #studyaesthetic)
- Willing to spend $30–$150 on a single ambient accessory if it looks beautiful and serves a mood
- Motivated by: creating a "vibe", ADHD focus aids, self-care rituals, content creation backgrounds

**Secondary — Gift Buyers (25–45)**
- Parents, partners, and friends buying gifts for desk enthusiasts
- High average order values ($60–$120 gift bundles)
- Peak demand: back-to-school (Aug–Sep), holiday season (Nov–Dec), Valentine's Day

**Tertiary — Remote Work Professionals (28–45)**
- Post-pandemic home office upgrade buyers
- More disposable income, prioritize ergonomics and aesthetics equally
- Respond to productivity and wellbeing framing

### Consumer Trends
- **Cozy core / cottagecore aesthetic** is replacing the sterile minimalist desk trend
- **Soft productivity** content is exploding: study-with-me, LoFi playlists, ASMR workspace videos
- Buyers increasingly research products through short-form video before purchasing
- Gen Z and Millennial buyers strongly prefer brands with a clear aesthetic identity and story
- Subscription box fatigue is opening space for curated one-time-purchase bundles

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1 — Soft Launch (Month 1–2)

**Goal:** Validate product-market fit and generate first 100 orders.

- Activate the newsletter capture (currently live on homepage) with a 15% first-order offer
- Seed product to 10–20 micro-influencers in the study/desk aesthetic niche (10k–100k followers) in exchange for honest reviews and UGC
- Launch on Instagram and Pinterest with 3 posts per week showcasing product in real desk setups
- Enable Google OAuth and email auth — remove all signup friction

**Key metrics:** Conversion rate, CAC from influencer seeding, email list growth

### Phase 2 — Paid Growth (Month 2–4)

**Goal:** Scale from 100 to 1,000 monthly orders.

- Launch Meta (Instagram/Facebook) ads targeting: desk setup audiences, study aesthetic, home office
- Pinterest promoted pins — high-intent visual search traffic
- TikTok creative: "rate my desk setup" reaction videos featuring duskglow products
- Google Shopping ads for product-specific searches ("ambient desk lamp", "desk organizer aesthetic")
- Retarget cart abandoners with a 10% discount (Supabase + email marketing integration)

**Target CAC:** < $18 | **Target ROAS:** 3.5x+

### Phase 3 — Community & Retention (Month 4+)

**Goal:** Build a recognizable brand with a loyal repeat-purchase base.

- Launch "Sanctuary Stories" — user-generated content series featuring customer desk setups
- Introduce seasonal limited-edition colorways (tied to the blush/lavender/sage palette)
- Build a rewards/loyalty program (points per purchase, bonus points for reviews and UGC)
- Holiday gift guide featuring duskglow bundles on relevant gift-guide blogs
- Expand into wholesale for boutique stationery and home décor stores
